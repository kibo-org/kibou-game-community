# English And Japanese

Source identifiers, comments, UI copy, error messages, documentation, Issues and
PRs use English or Japanese. Current runtime copy is English; this policy does not
claim that a Japanese UI or a language switch is already implemented.

Use meaningful English identifiers and natural English/Japanese explanations.
Review accessibility labels and document language when translating a screen.
Preserve spelling in required license and attribution notices; do not rewrite them
to satisfy a language filter. Use only fictional examples.

Japanese and Chinese share Han characters. A Unicode range cannot prove that a
sentence is Japanese rather than Chinese. Review language context manually;
do not delete Japanese Kanji or claim a character scan certifies the language.
This is a source/contribution policy, not a restriction on a player's identity.

コードと説明は英語または日本語に統一します。漢字の使用を一律に禁止しません。
翻訳では画面、ラベル、エラー、読み上げ用の文言をまとめて確認してください。
